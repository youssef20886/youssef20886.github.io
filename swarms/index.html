Swarms is a pixel-art roguelite auto-battler. Your units march on their own: your job is to build the deck, read the battlefield and make the calls that turn a losing fight around.

  • Command the swarm. Cards spawn soldiers all battle long. Knights hold the line, archers rain death from behind, assassins dive the back ranks.
  • A new road every run. A branching map of battles, elites, shops, treasure and strange "?" events.
  • Three enemy armies. The Restless Dead, the Orc Warlands and the Elven Wilds, each with its own boss.
  • Heroes turn the tide. Call in the Paladin, the Shadowblade, the Valkyrie and more.
  • Grow stronger every run. Permanent upgrades, unit and hero cards, new recruits.
  • Endless mode. Weekly and all-time leaderboards, plus a battle pass season.
  • Short battles, plays offline, no energy timers.
